# Be Light • I Am Light (प्रकाश स्वरूप बनो • मैं प्रकाश हूँ)

A mobile-friendly, meditative web experience designed for Diwali tables
and quiet reflection. Created for the Brahma Kumaris serving in
Vancouver, Surrey & Abbotsford, B.C., Canada.

**View it:** https://in8ly.github.io/I-am-light/

Generated in conversation by C.D. Good · @in8ly
(BK Charihey · Vancouver | Oregon)

## ✨ About the Experience
A peaceful digital getaway from the festival hustle into the light
itself. Visitors are invited to look quietly into a flickering diya
flame, steady their mind, and draw a personal Diwali virtue card from
8 bilingual virtues (Purity, Peace, Love, Joy, Power, Wisdom, Courage,
Simplicity).

## 🎶 Features
- Radiant autumn-leaf gradient and luminous glow
- Gently flickering diya flame
- Bilingual headings (English & Hindi)
- Draw a bilingual virtue card with a short reflection
- Play the "Come Into The Light" song by Bliss right on the page
- Donation link and event info (World Peace Meditation, 3rd Sunday
  of every month)

## 🗣️ Made through conversation
This page was made with conversational computing: built in the Gemini
app, with help from Claude and Copilot. No code was written by hand.
It's an example from in8ly of what becomes possible when you describe
what you want in plain language, look at the result, and keep refining
it together.

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Clone the repository:
   `git clone https://github.com/in8ly/I-am-light.git`
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.

## 🍴 Fork it
Other centres are welcome to fork this and adapt it for their own
Diwali tables. Please swap in your own centre's details, links, and
donation portal.
